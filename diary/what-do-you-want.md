# Diary: "What Do You Want?"

*The first video made with Nightshift. [Posted to X on October 28, 2026](https://x.com/hexeosis/status/2115545427290489287):*
*"new video "What Do You Want?" asking the only question nobody can forecast for you. fyi my p(doom) is the square root of -1"*

**The idea.** Everyone is forecasting the future: the weather turns, the numbers climb, every forecast a cage you didn't
choose. The song refuses the forecast and asks the only question nobody can answer for you. It loops: the last frame is the
first frame.

**The song.** Written as a brief and a lyric sheet, rendered locally with MiniMax Music 3 in ComfyUI. Seven movements:
The Seed, The Forecast, The Doom Futures, The Bloom, The Door (a rap verse), Quieting, The Return. Getting a second, male voice
for the rap took a casting note at the top of the caption and a named section tag; getting a clean, spacious vocal took
asking for reverb instead of "erosion". The keeper was one of four revision renders.

**The cast.** A singer and a rapper. Two worlds: real Los Angeles at night (a rooftop parking level, a
caged freeway bridge, a fenced basketball court in the rain, a 24-hour laundromat, the subway, a sunrise ridge) and a stage
with a giant glowing lattice of light.

**The HUD.** A forecast panel reading P(doom) that ticks up on every beat through the doom futures, scrambles when the chorus
blooms, and becomes a question mark. Lyrics that steer around faces, brackets that lock on to her face labelled
"SUBJECT 01 · UNFORECAST", a ticker counting choices made. Neon line-art illustrations of the futures people want land beside
her while she asks.

**The numbers.** 52 shots, 2:49, nine passes. One full pass is a night on an RTX 3090.

## The passes

- **Passes 1-3.** The pipeline and the video found each other: song-agnostic shot list, lip-sync anchors, the first cuts,
  the HUD, the grade contact sheets.
- **Pass 4.** Grade A+ (Portra at dusk with a touch more contrast). Rescue first/last-frame shots for faces that drifted. A
  pyramid fly-up, a meadow and a bioluminescent street for the ending. Animated illustrations.
- **Pass 5.** Erik's face fix on shot 3 with illustrations timed to her head turns; a self-consistent last frame killed a hair
  pop at the end of shot 15; the ending rebuilt as six one-bar cuts on the downbeats (coral reef revival, open-air workshop,
  a tree from winter to blossom are new); the pass number came off the HUD.
- **Pass 6.** Shot 3's face matched to shot 2 (0.28 -> 0.41); shot 21 matched to shot 22 (0.35 -> 0.56) with the original
  seed; the dance shot relit from the next shot so the red key carries into the close-up; a 60% crop as the reef's last frame
  makes the camera fly over it.
- **Pass 7.** No face box in the laundromat sequence; the workshop at normal speed; a new street to the horizon under the
  Milky Way.
- **Pass 8.** Erik's own frames for shots 3 and 21. A ComfyUI crash mid-render (out of memory), a restart, and on.
- **Pass 9.** Final. Shot 3 as first/last frame with the song: she looks at her raised hand as the robot and servers land
  beside her, turns to the lens and opens into Erik's shrug frame as the flower, DNA and ring arrive. A residential
  bioluminescent street with dog walkers and a kid on a bike, gliding forward under the Milky Way. Master 930 MB; posting copy
  305 MB.
