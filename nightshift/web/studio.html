NIGHTSHIFTstudio
ComfyUI…

A whole music video,
directed overnight on your own GPU.

Describe an idea and an agent writes the song, casts the characters and plans every shot, or build it step by step. Nightshift locks each character's look, paints every first frame, animates them with lip-sync on LTX 2.3, cuts on the beat and grades it, all in your local ComfyUI with open-weight models.

01

Your idea

As few or as many details as you like: the story, the places, who is in it, what the song is about. The agent writes the lyrics, the song brief, the characters and every shot, then Nightshift renders it.

01

Who's in it

A photo makes the character sheet from the picture; without one it's built from your description. You approve the look before anything else renders.

The grey text is only an example: type your own. Two performers or more places? Use the Agent tab.
Only use a photo of yourself or of someone who said yes. No celebrities.
02

The song

MiniMax Music 3 composes and sings it from your lyrics (open weights, running in ComfyUI), or bring your own track.

          What happens
          
    
            
  1. Character sheet first: front, back, face. You pick it.
    2. 
            
  2. Song, then beats and lyric timing, so every shot lands on a line.
    3. 
            
  3. First frames, then an identity edit from the sheet, so it's the same face in every shot.
    4. 
            
  4. LTX 2.3 animates each shot: lip-sync, camera moves, an establishing shot.
    5. 
            
  5. Takes are scored against the sheet, cut on the beat, graded.
    6. 
          

          Everything is a normal project on disk: the CLI and any agent over MCP can pick it up.
